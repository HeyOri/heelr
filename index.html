How it works

One tap. The drone does the rest.

01

Tap the flight plan

Your pens are mapped once. Starting a count after that is one tap. No pilot needed.

02

It flies itself

The drone takes off, flies a straight pass down every pen with the camera pointed straight down, and lands where it started.

03

AI counts every animal

The footage uploads and our model counts each animal and assigns it to the pen it stands in.

04

Review with proof

Per-pen inventory against expected counts, cattle positions on your satellite map, and video of each pen.
