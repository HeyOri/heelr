Questions farmers ask

Straight answers.

Does the drone spook the cattle?

No. Settled cattle ignore it. New intake may notice it and move around at first, but they get used to it quickly. It flies one straight pass down each pen, 15 to 45 metres up depending on the pen's width, and nobody has to walk in among the cattle.

What weather can it fly in?

Heelr flies a DJI Air 3S, which is rated for winds up to 12 m/s (about 43 km/h) and temperatures from −10 °C to 40 °C, with up to 45 minutes of flight per battery. If the wind gets too strong, the drone returns home and lands by itself. It can't fly in rain, so you fly once it clears.

Does it count cattle that are lying down or bunched at the bunk?

Yes. Our AI model is trained on cattle of every colour, standing or lying down, and bunched together at the bunk.

How does the footage get to Heelr?

After the drone lands, the footage uploads over your farm Wi-Fi and our AI counts every pen. If your internet is slow, you can buy a local hardware setup that only needs about 8 Mbps. Get in touch and we’ll tell you more.

How do my own numbers get into the dashboard?

Either way works: type the expected headcount for each pen into the dashboard, or import it from a spreadsheet. Every flight is checked against it.

What if a count looks wrong?

Every pen has its own video from the flight. Open it, watch the pass and check the animals yourself. Pens that need a second look are flagged with “Check count”.

Who owns and looks after the drone?

Your farm owns it. You buy a DJI Air 3S Fly More Combo from any certified DJI retailer or Takealot, and DJI handles any hardware warranty and repairs.

Who owns my data?

Your counts, videos and maps belong to your feedlot, and they’re kept on secure cloud storage.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your feedlot. Get in touch and we’ll send you a quote.
